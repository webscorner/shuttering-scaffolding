Steel and plywood shuttering can both produce accurate concrete work when designed, supported and installed correctly. The better choice depends on the structure, repetition, handling conditions and required finish.

## When steel shuttering works well

Steel shuttering plates are durable and suitable for repeated use. Their rigid construction helps maintain dimensions and can deliver a consistent finish when panels and joints remain in good condition.

Steel systems are especially useful where the same slab, wall or structural element will be repeated many times. The higher initial cost may be balanced by longer service life and faster reuse.

## When plywood offers flexibility

Plywood is easier to cut around unusual shapes, openings and changing dimensions. It can be practical for smaller projects or areas where the formwork arrangement changes frequently.

Its performance depends heavily on grade, thickness, edge protection, support spacing and site handling. Damaged surfaces or exposed edges can reduce the number of useful repetitions.

## Compare the complete cost

Do not compare panel price alone. Consider:

1. Expected number of repetitions.
2. Labour required for fabrication and fixing.
3. Crane or manual handling requirements.
4. Surface-finish expectations.
5. Repair, cleaning and storage arrangements.
