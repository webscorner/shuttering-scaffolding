ABOUT US

Welcome to Shuttering and Scaffolding—Your Reliable Construction Support Partner

We specialise in supplying quality shuttering and scaffolding material for modern construction projects. Our product range includes vertical standards, horizontal ledgers, U head jacks, base jacks, spigot pins, steel challi, props and supporting accessories.

From a single slab cycle to a complete access system, our team helps contractors select practical quantities and dependable components that keep work safe, accurate and on schedule.
